Polish Academy of Science - money distribution

05 August 2025

Recently I found and interesint data, namely how does the public money gets distributed among all the institues of Polish Academy of Sciences (PAN). Data is publicly available on the Government website.

Let's start with plotting each Institute on the map of Poland with the size related to the amount of money they get.

Some observations:

  • Clearly we see that the vast majority of Institutes are located in Warsaw. After Warsaw is Kraków, then Poznań, Wrocław, Gdańsk, Łódź, Lublin, Olsztyn, Białowieża, Gliwice, and Zabrze.
  • The largest Institute is Institute of Nuclear Physics in Kraków.
  • The smallest Institute is European Regional Centre for Ecohydrology in Łódź.
  • All but on Institutes of Humanities and Social Sciences are located in Warsaw.

Now lets take a look at the categories of the Institutes alltogether.

We can see the differences in finansing different categories. Humanities are not exeeding 20MLN while many technical institues are getting far more money.

It is also visible in the percent of institutes which got any increase at all. From humanities it was less than 30% while for technical institutes it was almost 70%.
