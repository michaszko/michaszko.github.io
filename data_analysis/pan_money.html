Polish Academy of Science - money distribution

05 August 2025

Recently I found and interesint data, namely how does the public money gets distributed among all the institues of Polish Academy of Sciences (PAN). Data is publicly available on the Government website (data for 2024 and 2025).

Let's start with plotting each Institute on the map of Poland with the size related to the amount of money they got in 2025 and the color related to the category it belongs to. We will have five categories:

  • I – Humanities and Social Sciences
  • II – Biological and Farmer Sciences
  • III – Exact Sciences and Geological Sciences
  • IV – Technical Sciences
  • V – Medical Sciences

We can now make some observations:

  • We can clearly see that the vast majority of Institutes are located in Warsaw. After it there is Kraków, then Poznań, Wrocław, Gdańsk, Łódź, Lublin, Olsztyn, Białowieża, Gliwice, and Zabrze.
  • The largest Institute is Institute of Nuclear Physics in Kraków.
  • There is plenty of small Institutes, but the smallest Institute is European Regional Centre for Ecohydrology in Łódź.
  • All but on Institutes of Humanities and Social Sciences are located in Warsaw.
  • Only Institutes of Biological and Farmer Sciences are located outisde of big cities.

Looking at the goegraphic location does not allow us to compare different catogories. So lets take a look at all the Institutes split in different categories.

Now many thing become more clear. Most notably there is no institute of Humanities and Social Sciences in Poland that exides 20MLN PLN. This is in high contrast with other catogories where many institutes get more than that. The possible reason for it is that other institutes are using their money not only for salaries for their scientis but also for buying expensive aparatures/utilities.

What is also interesting is the gap in the III category (Exact Sciences and Geological Sciences). The poorest institute in this category is having more money than many other institutes in other categories. Our initial guess on why is that could be that you need more money on apparature and also the quality of those institutes is high. Let us dive deeper if that is true claim.

Lets take a look at the change of the amount of money between 2024 and 2025 in each category.

The official communicate says that the incrase was 5% and it was highest ever [1]. We can see that, even if on average it is the case, the truth is more complex. Looking at different categories separatelly we see that Humanities and Social Sciences got only 2% increase, while Biological and Farmer Sciences got more than 7%. Other categories are close to the average value of 5%.

This information is quite bizzare. Least funded category got the lowest increase. Humanties seems to be a victim of Mathew effect. But the final effect will be that those institutes slowly die out. Maybe its the results that the rulers dont see studing humanities as a worth to a polish society. But I think its really short sighted thinking.

Browsing through the data I have noticed that many of the Institutes did not get any increase at all. Lets now quantify this. Plot bellow shows the percent of institutes in a given category which got the increase.

Only 30% of Institutes in the Humanities and Social Sciences category got the increase. On the other hand almost 70% of Institutes from the Biological and Farmer Sciences category got the increase. The other categories are somewhere in the middle. It expalins why the avearage change was so small in the first catoegry. Vast majority of them did not get any increase.

Let us look even deeper. The plot below shows the percent change for each institute but this time we categorized it with the average scientific score (the higher the better) [2].

Lets break down this plot. First on the x-axis there is a score of each Institute. The higher the score the better the Institute is. The highest score is A+ [more details]. On y-axis there is a percent change of the money between 2024-2025. Maximum increase was 10%. The rules for the increase are well defined in the [ustawa], and the final score depeneds on many conditions. Most of them are not public (especially those related to the personel) but at least one is public, which is this score. So we can plot this scatter plot. What we see is that there is a corelation -- the higher the score the bigger the increease. But there is maaaany exceptions. There are insitutts whcih have a score of B+ and got maximum increase and those who have A+ which got 0% increase. What is interesting is that most of the institutes are in the cateogry of A and most of them did not get any increase.
