Research

I am currently doing my PhD in quantum machine learning at the University of Warsaw. More specifically I focus on the time series prediction using quantum enhanced machine learning.

During my bachelor I have worked on a Monte Carlo simulation of neutrino collisions [LINK], and during my master I have been working on the dynamics of open quantum systems.
