Research Overview

I am a PhD candidate in quantum machine learning at the University of Warsaw (Faculty of Mathematics, Informatics and Mechanics). My research focuses on photonic quantum computing architectures, reservoir computing with quantum memristors, and quantum algorithms for time-series forecasting.
